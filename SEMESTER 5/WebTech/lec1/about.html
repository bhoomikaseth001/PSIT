About Our Institute

Pranveer Singh Institute of Technology (PSIT) is a premier institute dedicated to excellence in education, research, and innovation. Established with a vision to nurture future leaders, PSIT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, management, and computer applications.

To be a center of excellence in technical and professional education, recognized for its contribution to industry and society.
